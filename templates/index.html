How to read this result

Scores summarize matched local URL patterns.

Risk bands

  • Low (0–24): few configured patterns matched; not a safety verdict.
  • Medium (25–49): one or more patterns should be reviewed.
  • High (50–100): multiple or stronger patterns matched; investigate independently.

Patterns include non-HTTPS URLs, embedded credentials, IP-address hosts, punycode, long or deeply nested hostnames, and selected URL keywords.
